# 16 · The analysis checklist

The procedure the engine automates. Use it by hand for anything the engine can't
see (news, earnings dates, sector, breadth).

## 0. Context (manual)
- [ ] What is the broad market doing? (Index above or below its 200-day? Breadth?)
- [ ] Sector trend? Is the stock leading or lagging its sector?
- [ ] **Earnings or major events in the next 2–3 weeks?** If so, gap risk overrides your stop.
- [ ] Liquidity: can you exit your full size in a few minutes without moving the price?

## 1. Trend (highest weight)
- [ ] Weekly: price vs 40-week MA, and its slope (Weinstein stage)
- [ ] Daily: price vs 50 and 200 SMA, the 50/200 regime, 200-SMA slope
- [ ] Structure: HH/HL or LH/LL from swing pivots
- [ ] ADX > 20? If not, discount every trend signal
- [ ] Ichimoku cloud position, Supertrend, SAR (supporting evidence only)

## 2. Momentum
- [ ] RSI range (40–80 bull / 20–60 bear), not just 30/70
- [ ] MACD vs signal and zero; histogram slope
- [ ] 3-month ROC
- [ ] Divergences at the last two swing points (warning only)

## 3. Volume
- [ ] OBV direction agrees with price?
- [ ] CMF / MFI: accumulation or distribution?
- [ ] Up-day vs down-day volume
- [ ] Breakout bar volume ≥ 1.5× average?

## 4. Volatility
- [ ] ATR% (sets the stop and the size)
- [ ] Squeeze? (direction unknown; wait for release)
- [ ] Volatility regime percentile: extreme HV means cut size

## 5. Structure & levels
- [ ] Nearest support and resistance (with touch counts)
- [ ] 52-week high/low position
- [ ] Confirmed patterns only; note measured targets
- [ ] Fibonacci and gaps as confluence only

## 6. Relative strength
- [ ] 3- and 6-month return vs benchmark
- [ ] RS line trend

## 7. Trade plan
- [ ] Direction agrees with the higher-timeframe trend?
- [ ] Entry trigger defined (breakout close / pullback hold / reclaim)
- [ ] Structural stop + ATR buffer; ≤ 3 ATR
- [ ] Target at the next opposing level; R:R ≥ 2 preferred, ≥ 1.5 minimum
- [ ] Size = (account × risk%) ÷ stop distance; check the position value cap
- [ ] Invalidation written down in one sentence

## 8. Roast it
- [ ] Is the move already extended (> 2.5 ATR above the 50-day)?
- [ ] Are pillars contradicting each other?
- [ ] Did this signal ever work on this stock (evidence table)?
- [ ] Is the stock beating the index? If not, why not buy the index?
- [ ] What would make you exit? If you can't answer, don't enter.
