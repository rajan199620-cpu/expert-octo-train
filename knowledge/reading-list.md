# Reading list (primary sources)

These are the books and papers this knowledge base summarises. They are copyrighted
and are **not** included in this repo. Buy them, borrow them, or read the papers
through the authors' sites, SSRN or your library.

## Core textbooks

| Book | Author | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| *Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets* | John J. Murphy | The standard all-round reference |
| *Technical Analysis of Stock Trends* | Edwards & Magee (later eds. Bassetti) | The original chart-pattern bible |
| *Technical Analysis Explained* | Martin Pring | Cycles, momentum, intermarket |
| *Technical Analysis: The Complete Resource for Financial Market Technicians* | Kirkpatrick & Dahlquist | The CMT curriculum textbook; most rigorous |
| *Encyclopedia of Chart Patterns* | Thomas Bulkowski | Statistics on pattern performance and failure rates |
| *Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques* | Steve Nison | Candlesticks |
| *New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems* | J. Welles Wilder | RSI, ATR, ADX, Parabolic SAR (original source) |
| *Technical Analysis: Power Tools for Active Investors* | Gerald Appel | MACD from its creator |
| *Bollinger on Bollinger Bands* | John Bollinger | Bands, %B, bandwidth |
| *Intermarket Analysis* | John J. Murphy | Bonds/stocks/commodities/currencies |

## Methods and systems

| Book | Author | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| *Secrets for Profiting in Bull and Bear Markets* | Stan Weinstein | Stage analysis |
| *How to Make Money in Stocks* | William O'Neil | CAN SLIM, cup & handle, RS |
| *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard* | Mark Minervini | Trend template, VCP |
| *Trading for a Living* / *The New Trading for a Living* | Alexander Elder | Triple screen, psychology, the 2% and 6% rules |
| *Way of the Turtle* | Curtis Faith | Donchian trend-following system |
| *Elliott Wave Principle* | Frost & Prechter | Elliott |
| *Trades About to Happen* / *Wyckoff 2.0* | David Weis / Rubén Villahermosa | Wyckoff |
| *Technical Analysis for the Trading Professional* | Constance Brown | RSI ranges, advanced oscillators |
| *Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom* | Van Tharp | R-multiples, position sizing, expectancy |

## Evidence-based / critical

| Work | Author(s) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Evidence-Based Technical Analysis* | David Aronson | Data-snooping, scientific method applied to TA |
| "Simple Technical Trading Rules and the Stochastic Properties of Stock Returns" (J. Finance 1992) | Brock, Lakonishok & LeBaron | Classic MA and breakout evidence |
| "Data-Snooping, Technical Trading Rule Performance, and the Bootstrap" (J. Finance 1999) | Sullivan, Timmermann & White | Corrects for data-mining |
| "Foundations of Technical Analysis" (J. Finance 2000) | Lo, Mamaysky & Wang | Objective pattern recognition |
| "What Do We Know About the Profitability of Technical Analysis?" (J. Econ. Surveys 2007) | Park & Irwin | Survey of the literature |
| "Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers" (J. Finance 1993) | Jegadeesh & Titman | Momentum |
| "Time Series Momentum" (JFE 2012) | Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen | Trend-following |
| "A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following Investing" (2017) | Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen | Long-run trend evidence |
| "The 52-Week High and Momentum Investing" (J. Finance 2004) | George & Hwang | 52-week-high effect |
| "Does Academic Research Destroy Stock Return Predictability?" (J. Finance 2016) | McLean & Pontiff | Edge decay after publication |
| "The Adaptive Markets Hypothesis" (JPM 2004) | Andrew Lo | Why edges come and go |
| *A Random Walk Down Wall Street* | Burton Malkiel | The strongest sceptical case |
| *Fooled by Randomness* | Nassim Taleb | Luck vs skill |
